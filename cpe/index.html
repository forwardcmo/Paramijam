WHO SHOULD ATTEND
This event is designed for SaaS and cloud companies pursuing or maintaining federal authorizations, including FedRAMP Rev5, FedRAMP 20x, agency ATOs under RMF and DoD impact levels; federal, state and local government staff involved in cloud security authorization; 3PAOs, consultants and partners in the FedRAMP ecosystem; and security, compliance and GRC professionals. Prior experience with FedRAMP is not required. Continuous authorization, AI-assisted evidence collection and GRC engineering best practices will be discussed. Others are welcome.
