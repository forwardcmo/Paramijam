> FAQ

Have questions? Of course you do.

Is my ticket really covered?+

Yes. Claim it through your invite link by Dec 15 and Paramify pays for your Paramijam pass, including Monday's sessions.

What happens if I miss Dec 15?+

Your reserved ticket goes back into the public drop. You can still buy one at the public price while tickets last. See public tickets.

Are travel and lodging covered?+

Your ticket is covered; travel and lodging are not. Flights are booked on your own. For lodging, book through our link to get a discounted rate at the Pendry Park City. Book your room.

When should I arrive?+

Sunday, Jan 24. Monday morning is open for winter activity fittings and 1:1s with the Paramify team, sessions run 1:00 to 6:00 PM, and the Welcome Event is 7 to 10 PM in the Pendry Lounge. Paramijam runs Tuesday through Thursday.

Who's in the room on Monday?+

Customer Advisory Board members and Club 7739 partners share the sessions from 1:00 to 4:30. At 4:45 the Advisory Board and the Partner Summit meet separately, and everyone comes back together for the Welcome Event.

What if I can't make Monday?+

Please let your CSM or partner manager know ahead of time so we can make accommodations.

Do I need to prepare anything?+

We'll send a short pre-read by Jan 6. The roadmap focus groups run by role, so come ready to talk about how your team works today.

Are Monday's sessions confidential?+

Yes. Monday's sessions, including roadmap previews, fall under the NDA you already have with us as an Advisory Board member or partner.

Need sign-off to travel?+

We wrote a justification letter covering CPE credits, deliverables and cost. Download the letter.

Who do I talk to?+

Reply to your invite or reach your Paramify contact directly. Anything else: paramijam@paramify.com.
