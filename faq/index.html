> TICKETS + PAYMENT
What are drops?+

Tickets are on sale every day. Drops are limited windows with their own batch of tickets and their own perks. Drop 2 runs Nov 17–30 with 100 tickets, with 50% off General Admission, Preferred and VIP (Black isn’t discounted). See all drops.

Can I buy now and pay later?+

Yes. Choose “Pay later by invoice” at checkout. Your seat is held right away and we email you an invoice you can pay online. Payment is due in full by January 15, 2027, and we’ll send reminders before then. Pay later is available through January 4; the final drop is pay-now only.

What if my invoice isn’t paid?+

An invoice order is a commitment to attend and pay. Unpaid invoices aren’t cancelled automatically and remain due after January 15, whether or not you attend. If you need to cancel, email us by December 30.

Can I get a refund or transfer my ticket?+

Yes, until December 30, 2026. Email paramijam@paramify.com by then for a full refund or to cancel your invoice. After December 30, all sales are final, but you can still transfer your ticket to a teammate by emailing us their name.

Can I buy for my team?+

Yes. Checkout supports up to 10 tickets per order, and you can mix tiers. Fill in everyone’s details now, or just the basics and send your team a link to finish later.

Can we pay with a PO?+

Choose pay later at checkout, then reply to your invoice email with your PO number or any paperwork your finance team needs.

Do I need a discount code for Drop 2?+

No. The 50% discount on General Admission, Preferred and VIP is applied automatically at checkout while Drop 2 is open. Black tickets aren’t part of the Drop 2 discount. If you already have a Drop 2 code, you don’t need it: discounts don’t stack, and you’ll always get the best one available.

> THE EVENT
When and where is Paramijam?+

January 26–28, 2027, at the Pendry Park City in Park City, Utah. Plan to arrive Monday, January 25.

Who is it for?+

GRC engineers, security and compliance leaders, CISOs, assessors, partners and federal teams working toward or maintaining federal ATOs: FedRAMP Rev5, FedRAMP 20x, agency ATOs under RMF, and DoD impact levels.

What’s on the agenda?+

Keynotes and panels Tuesday, three tracks of breakouts Wednesday, and hands-on workshops Thursday, with winter activities in the mornings. See the agenda.

What’s included in each tier?+

Every tier gets keynotes, panels, breakouts, 8–13 CPE credit hours and the concert. Preferred, VIP and Black add Thursday workshops and two days of winter activities. VIP adds reserved seating and the lounge. Black adds VIP concert access and airport transport. Compare tiers.

How do CPE credits work?+

8–13 CPE credit hours are available, based on registration type and the sessions you attend, administered by Carahsoft. CPE credit details.

Is the event accessible?+

Tell us what you need during registration and our team will follow up before the event. Every session is open to all attendees; winter activities are optional.

> TRAVEL + STAY
Where do I stay?+

We hold a room block at the Pendry Park City at a discounted nightly rate. See the hotel or book your room. The link is also in your confirmation email.

Which airport should I fly into?+

Salt Lake City International (SLC), about 45 minutes from Park City. Black tickets include VIP airport transport.
