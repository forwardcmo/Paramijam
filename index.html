What's included in each tier?+

Every tier gets keynotes, panels, breakouts and 8+ CPE credits. Preferred, VIP and Black add Thursday workshops and two days of winter activities. VIP adds reserved seating and the lounge. Black adds VIP concert access and airport transport. Compare tiers.

Can I buy for my team?+

Yes. Registration supports group checkout for multiple attendees in a single order.

Can we pay by invoice or PO?+

Yes. Choose card or invoice / PO at checkout.

We are pleased to offer up to 8+ continuing professional education (CPE) credits to those that attend the event. For more information on the CPE credit we are offering, our CPE sponsor (Carahsoft), and the submission process, please visit our CPE Credit page.
